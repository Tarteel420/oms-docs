## Sources

This guide is based on:

1. A read-only walkthrough of the live EZ-OMS application (every sidebar page, its forms and dialogs), September 2026.
2. The EZ-OMS product demo (23 Sep 2026).
3. The EZ-OMS Software Requirements Specification (SRS v1.0, 10 Jan 2026).

Where they differ, the guide follows the **live application**.

## Differences between the SRS and the live app

| Topic | SRS | Live application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Order statuses** | Draft, Submitted, Shipped, Received, Completed, Cancelled | 19 statuses — see [Order Statuses](/docs/orders/order-statuses) |
| **Roles** | Admin, Distributor, Reseller, Warehouse Manager, Customer | superadmin, admin, manager, user — see [User Roles](/docs/getting-started/user-roles) |
| **Kit receiving** | Warehouse scans kits in EZ-OMS on arrival | **Scan ID** marks kits *assembled* before shipping; received products are recorded in GreenLoop |
| **Certificates** | Generated for received and processed orders | Issued per container on **Certificate of Recycling**; customers can download from `/recycle` |
| **Reports** | Kit Distribution, Return Tracking, Warehouse Receipt, Recycling Metrics, Warehouse Operations | Shipping Analytics, Safety-Kleen, Consolidated Invoice, Recycling Annual, Certificate of Recycling |

## Features found in the app that the SRS doesn't describe

- [Auto-Reorder](/docs/management/auto-reorder) per distributor.
- [Complaints](/docs/support) ticketing.
- [Scan Kits](/docs/orders/scan-kits) assembly scanning.
- Invoicing with **Ready to Invoice** and **Invoices Report**.
- Distributor logins created from the Distributors page.
- The [ShipNest Integration](/docs/resources/integrations#shipnest-integration-page) settings page.

## Open questions

- **Product list:** Product Management showed **No products found** during the walkthrough, although the Dashboard counts more than 5,000 products.
- **Status rules:** what moves an order automatically from one status to the next isn't documented.
- **Role permissions:** which pages each role can open isn't shown in the app.
- **Customer self-service:** the checkout pages exist, but the customer view wasn't available to the walkthrough account.
- **Shipping Analytics:** report columns weren't captured, because a report wasn't generated during the walkthrough.

## Screenshots
