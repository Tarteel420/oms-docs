---
title: Shipment Tracking
description: Follow a bundle's shipment from the order page.
---

Each bundle in [Order Details](/docs/orders/order-details) has a **Shipment Tracking** area.

## What you see

- The latest tracking event, for example **Label Created — Shipper created a label, UPS has not received the package yet**, with date and time.
- **Track on UPS** — opens the carrier's tracking page.
- **Show history** — shows earlier tracking events.

<Callout type="info">
  Live tracking updates are available for **UPS** shipments only. For other carriers the page shows the tracking number without live events.
</Callout>

## Find an order by tracking number

On the [order list](/docs/orders/order-list), enter the number in the **Tracking Number** filter.

## Tracking and invoicing

Orders can be invoiced once tracking shows the shipment is moving. See [Invoicing](/docs/reports/invoicing).
