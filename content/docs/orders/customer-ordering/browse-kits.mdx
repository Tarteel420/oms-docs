---

Customers browse the recycling kits their **distributor** offers. Only active kits are shown, with the distributor's pricing.

## Browse the catalog

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the kit catalog

Open the kit browsing page. All active kit types from your distributor are listed, 20 per page.
</Step>
<Step>
### Narrow the list

- **Filter by category** to see one kind of kit.
- **Search** by kit code or name to find a specific kit.
</Step>
<Step>
### Review a kit

Open a kit to see its description and price.
</Step>
</Steps>
