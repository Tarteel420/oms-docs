After [browsing kits](/docs/orders/customer-ordering/browse-kits), customers add them to a cart and check out.

## Place an order

<Steps>
<Step>
### Add kits to your cart

Add each kit type you need and choose the quantity.
</Step>
<Step>
### Review your cart

Open the cart to review the summary and pricing. You can **add**, **remove** or **change the quantity** of any kit.
</Step>
<Step>
### Proceed to checkout

Go to checkout. Your address is **filled in automatically** from your account — check it is correct.
</Step>
<Step>
### Submit the order

Submit the order.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

1. The order is created (the SRS describes it as a draft order).
2. An **order confirmation** page is shown.
3. An order confirmation **email** is sent to you.

The order is then prepared and shipped like any other order — see [The Order Lifecycle](/docs/getting-started/order-lifecycle).

<Callout type="info">
  Your cart is saved in your browser, so items stay in it if you leave the page and come back on the same device.
</Callout>
