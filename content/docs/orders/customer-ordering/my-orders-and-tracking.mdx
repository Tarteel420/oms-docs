Customers can view their past orders and follow each return kit on its way back to the warehouse.

## View your orders

1. Open **My Orders**.
2. Your kit order history is listed. Orders are read-only.
3. Open an order to see its bundles and kits.

## Track your return kits

For each kit in an order you can see:

- Its **tracking number**.
- Its current **status** (the SRS lists Labeled, In Transit, Received and Processed).
- Live tracking information from ShipNest.
- Confirmation when the warehouse has **received** the kit, and when **processing** is complete.



## Notifications

You can receive a notification when a kit has been received at the warehouse, and a completion confirmation when the whole order has been processed.
