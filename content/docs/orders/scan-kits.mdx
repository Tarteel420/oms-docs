The **Scan ID** page records kit assembly. Scanning a kit's Unique ID marks it **assembled** and updates the order's progress.

## Open the scan page

Any of these open **Scan ID**:

- **Scan Kits** on Orders Management.
- **Scan Kits** in an order's **Bundles & Kits** section.
- The green floating **Scan** button on the right edge of every page.

## Scan a kit

<Steps>
<Step>
### Check the scanner details

**Scanned by** is filled in with your logged-in name. Enter the **Scan station / location** — it is saved in your browser for your next scans.
</Step>
<Step>
### Scan the Unique ID

- With a **handheld barcode scanner**: click in **Manual Unique ID** and scan.
- With a **camera**: select **Open Camera**.
- By hand: type the kit ID into **Manual Unique ID**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Submit

Select **Submit**. The outcome appears under **Last Scan Result**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Scan history

**Scan History** lists scan events for **Today** or the last **7 Days**. Use the refresh icon to update it.
