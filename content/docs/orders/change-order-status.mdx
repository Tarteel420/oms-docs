---
title: Change Order Status
description: Move an order to a different status.
---

## From the order list

In the **Status** column of the [order list](/docs/orders/order-list), open the dropdown on the order's status chip and choose the new status.

## From the order page

On [Order Details](/docs/orders/order-details), open the status dropdown under the order number and choose from: **Ready to Print**, **Printed**, **In Assembly**, **Partial Assembly**, **Ready to Ship**, **Shipped**, **Completed**, **Partial Return** or **Cancelled**.

## Revert a shipped order

Shipped orders show **Revert to Ready to Ship** — in the **Actions** column of the list and at the top of the order page. It moves the order back to **Ready to Ship**.

<Callout type="warn">
  Status changes take effect as soon as you select them. Check you have the right order open before changing its status.
</Callout>
