---
title: Add a Product
description: Create a recycling kit product for a distributor.
---

1. Open **Products** in the sidebar.
2. Select **Add Product**. The **Add New Product** form opens.

<Screenshot
  file="products/add-product.png"
  title="Add New Product"
  description="The Add New Product form with product code, descriptions, prices, distributor, category and image upload."
/>

## Fields

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Product Code** | Yes | The SKU, for example `35BT`. |
| **Short Description** | Yes | Shown as the product name. |
| **Long Description** | Yes | Full description. |
| **List Price** | Yes | Standard price. |
| **Negotiated Price** | Yes | The distributor's price. |
| **Distributor** | Yes | Which distributor the product belongs to. |
| **Category** | No | Defaults to **No Category**. See [Product Categories](/docs/products/product-categories). |
| **Upload Image** | No | A picture of the kit. |

3. Select **Create** to save, or **Cancel** to close.

## What happens next

The product becomes available for that distributor's orders — see [Create an Order](/docs/orders/create-an-order) — and can be included in the distributor's [auto-reorder](/docs/management/auto-reorder) settings.

<Callout type="info">
  To offer the same kit to another distributor, add it again for that distributor with its own prices.
</Callout>
