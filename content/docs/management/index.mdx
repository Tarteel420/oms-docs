---
title: Account Hierarchy
description: How distributors, resellers and customers relate to each other.
---

EZ-OMS organizes the businesses it serves into tiers. Every order belongs to a **distributor**, and optionally a **reseller**, and is shipped to a **customer**.

## The tiers

```
Distributor        e.g. a large recycling company
 ├── Reseller      sells kits on to its own customers
 │    └── Customer
 └── Customer      the end user who fills and returns kits
```

For example, a large distributor such as American Lamp Recycling provides kits to its customers and resellers, and the resellers sell them on further.

| Account | Created by | Can manage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Distributor** | Admin | Its own resellers, customers, orders and product pricing. Can be given its own login. |
| **Reseller** | Distributor or Admin | Its own customers and their orders. |
| **Customer** | Distributor or Admin | Its own account; can view its own orders. |

## Data is kept separate

Each account only sees its own data:

- Distributors cannot see other distributors' data.
- Customers only see their own orders and kits.
- Admins can see all data.
