Customers don't need an EZ-OMS login to get their certificate. The public **Certificate of Recycling (COR)** page is at `/recycle` on the portal.

<Screenshot
  file="resources/public-cor-lookup.png"
  title="Public COR lookup"
  description="The public Certificate of Recycling page with the Unique Container # field and Find kit button."
/>

## How customers use it

1. Open the page.
2. Enter the **Unique Container #** printed on the shipping label of the return kit (for example `9136105`).
3. Select **Find kit** to download the COR and see the kit's status.

The page also lists the kit types recycled (straight lamps, CFL/other lamps, batteries, ballasts, mercury devices, smoke detectors, electronic waste, signs) and links to ezontheearth.com.
