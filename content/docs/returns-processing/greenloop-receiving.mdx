OMS holds the **order and tracking records**. The **receiving** side — recording products as they physically arrive — is tracked in **GreenLoop**, the system used by the warehouse.

## How the two systems work together

| System | Records |
| --- | --- |
| **OMS** | Orders, bundles, return kits and tracking numbers. |
| **GreenLoop** | Products received at the warehouse. |

GreenLoop and OMS are **connected**. Products marked as received in GreenLoop can then be invoiced to the customer. See [Invoicing](/docs/reports/invoicing).

## Record received products

1. In GreenLoop, open the **Receiving** area.
2. Mark the products that have been received.
