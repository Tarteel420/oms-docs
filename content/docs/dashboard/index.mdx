The **Dashboard** is the first page you see after [logging in](/docs/getting-started/logging-in). It summarizes the state of the business so you can spot what needs attention without opening each module.

## Open the Dashboard

1. Select **Dashboard** under **Overview** in the sidebar.
2. The Dashboard loads with the latest figures for the data your role can see.

## What's on the Dashboard

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Summary cards** | Total Orders, Total Distributors, Total Products, Total Revenue and Avg. Order Value. |
| **Orders Over Time** | A chart of order volume over time. |
| **Orders by Status** | A chart of how many orders are in each status. |
| **Recent Orders** | The latest orders with their order ID, customer, date, total and status. |
| **Top Products by Price** | The highest-priced products with their product code, description, list price and category. |

For a closer look at each section, see [Reading the Dashboard](/docs/dashboard/reading-the-dashboard).

<Callout title="What you see depends on your role">
  Like all pages in OMS, the Dashboard only includes data your account is allowed to see. See [User Roles](/docs/getting-started/user-roles).
</Callout>
