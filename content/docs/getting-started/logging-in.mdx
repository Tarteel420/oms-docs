You need a user account to use OMS. Accounts are created by an administrator — see [User Management](/docs/management/user-management).

## Sign in

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the OMS login page

Open the OMS portal (`portal.ezontheearth.com`) in a supported browser: Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge.
</Step>
<Step>
### Enter your credentials

Enter your **username or email** and your **password**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Sign in

Submit the form. After a successful login, the [Dashboard](/docs/dashboard) opens.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Log out

Select your initial (the **Account** button) at the top right, then select **Logout**. The menu also shows your username and email.

## Sessions and automatic logout

- A session lasts **30 minutes** by default.
- When your session expires, you are **logged out automatically** and must sign in again.

<Callout type="warn">
  Save your work regularly. Unsaved changes may be lost when your session expires.
</Callout>

## Forgotten password

OMS includes a **password reset** page. Password reset messages are sent by email.

## Password rules

Passwords must be **at least 8 characters** long and meet complexity requirements.

## Access denied

If you open a page your role does not allow, OMS shows an **Access denied** page. Contact your administrator if you believe you need access. See [User Roles](/docs/getting-started/user-roles).

## Supported devices

OMS is a web application that works on desktop, tablet and mobile browsers.
