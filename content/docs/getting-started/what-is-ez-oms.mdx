EZ-OMS is the in-house **order management system** used to run recycling kit orders. It manages the full journey of a recycling kit — from the moment an order comes in, to shipping an empty kit to the customer, to receiving the filled kit back at a warehouse.

## Who uses EZ-OMS

EZ-OMS is used mainly for the recycling programs of **Lighting Resources (LRI)** and **Easy on the Earth**. Easy on the Earth acts as the customer-facing brand for LRI.

The system serves a large network of accounts — more than a hundred distributors and thousands of customers — together with the internal staff who manage their orders.

## How EZ-OMS works with other systems

EZ-OMS does not work alone. It connects to:

- **Amazon and Shopify** — orders placed on these marketplaces flow into EZ-OMS automatically.
- **Email and the Safety-Kleen mail-back system** — orders arriving by email are brought in automatically.
- **ShipNest** — generates shipping labels and tracks shipments.
- **GreenLoop** — used by the warehouse to record kits that are received.

See [Integrations](/docs/resources/integrations) for details.
