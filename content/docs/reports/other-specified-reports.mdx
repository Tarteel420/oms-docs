EZ-OMS contains more reports than are shown in the sidebar. Only the most-used reports are currently enabled. The requirements define the following additional reports.

<Callout type="warn" title="Availability">
  These reports are specified in the requirements. They may not be enabled in your environment. Check the **Reports** section of your sidebar to see what is available to you.
</Callout>

## Kit Distribution Report

Analyzes how kits are distributed.

- Total kits distributed by type.
- Orders by status.
- Kits by date range, distributor or customer.
- Distribution trends over time.
- **Filters:** date, distributor, status, kit type.
- **Export:** Excel, CSV.

## Kit Return Tracking Report

Monitors return rates.

- Return kits by status (pending, in transit, received, processed).
- Average return time by warehouse.
- Kit completion rates.
- **Filters:** date, distributor, warehouse, status.
- **Export:** Excel.

## Warehouse Receipt Report

- Kits received by warehouse or location.
- Daily receipt counts.
- Kit types received.
- Processing completion rates.
- **Filters:** date, warehouse, kit type.

## Recycling Metrics Report

Tracks recycling performance.

- Total weight of recyclables processed.
- Kit fill rates by type.
- Recycling completion overview.
- Environmental impact summary.
- **Filters:** date range, distributor, kit type.
- **Export:** PDF, Excel.

## Warehouse Operations Report

Tracks processing efficiency for Warehouse Managers.

- Daily and weekly operations summary.
- Kit processing throughput.
- Storage and inventory tracking.
- Staff efficiency metrics.
- **Export:** Excel, CSV.
