---
title: Invoicing
description: Invoice orders once their shipment is moving, and review past invoices.
---

Select **Invoicing** under **Overview**. The page has two tabs: **Ready to Invoice** and **Invoices Report**.

<Callout type="info" title="Amazon and Shopify">
  Amazon and Shopify orders are billed on those platforms and are excluded from Invoicing.
</Callout>

## Ready to Invoice tab

Lists orders whose shipment is moving and are ready to bill. The count (for example **404 ready**) is shown at the top right.

<Screenshot
  file="reports/invoicing-ready.png"
  title="Invoicing — Ready to Invoice"
  description="The Ready to Invoice tab with search, the Ready date filter, the ready count, Mark Invoiced, and the list of orders with an Invoice button on each row."
/>

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Order #** | The order number. |
| **Customer** | Who to bill. |
| **PO #** | The customer's PO number, or **—**. |
| **Ready** | How long the order has been ready, for example **4d ago**. |
| **Total** | The order value. |
| **Invoice** | The **Invoice…** button. |

Filter with **Search customer, PO, order #…** and the **Ready date** range.

### Invoice one order

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the invoice panel

Select **Invoice…** on the order's row. The **Invoice — Order #…** panel opens on the right.
</Step>
<Step>
### Enter the invoice details

- **Invoice #** — the invoice number.
- **Document date** — defaults to today.
- **Email status** — **Not sent** or **Sent**.
- **C.A. Remarks** — optional remarks.
</Step>
<Step>
### Save

Select **Save & Mark Invoiced**. The order leaves the Ready to Invoice list and appears in the Invoices Report.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot
  file="reports/invoice-drawer.png"
  title="Invoice panel"
  description="The Invoice — Order panel with Invoice #, Document date, Email status and C.A. Remarks, and the Save & Mark Invoiced button."
/>

### Mark several orders invoiced

Tick the checkboxes for the orders, then select **Mark Invoiced**.

### Invoice before tracking confirms movement

On an order's page, the **Invoicing** card shows **Awaiting tracking** until tracking confirms the shipment is moving. Select **Add invoice** to invoice it straight away. See [Order Details](/docs/orders/order-details).

## Invoices Report tab

Shows invoices that have been generated.

<Screenshot
  file="reports/invoices-report.png"
  title="Invoicing — Invoices Report"
  description="The Invoices Report tab with the All / Last 24h / Last 7 days / Custom range filters, the total amount and invoice count, and the invoice list."
/>

- Filter with **All**, **Last 24h**, **Last 7 days** or a **Custom range**.
- The total amount and number of invoices for the period are shown at the top right.

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Order #** | The order. |
| **Customer** | Who was billed. |
| **Invoice #** | The invoice number. |
| **Doc Date** | The document date. |
| **Email** | Email status, for example **Sent**. |
| **C.A. Remarks** | Remarks, for example **Created**. |
| **Invoiced By** | The user who invoiced it. |
| **Amount** | The invoiced amount. |
