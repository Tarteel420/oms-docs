---
title: Recycling Annual Report
description: Yearly summary of products ordered and recycling returns, with weights.
---

Select **Recycling Annual** under **Reports**.

## Run the report

1. Choose the **Distributor**.
2. Enter the **Year**.
3. **Include legacy NULLs** — **Yes** or **No** (whether to include older records with missing values).
4. **Return kits date** — which date decides the year a return kit counts in: **Created Date**, **Shipped Date** or **Delivery Date**.
5. The report appears on the page. Select **Export to Excel** to download it.

Until a distributor and year are chosen, the page shows *Select a distributor and a year to view the report.*
