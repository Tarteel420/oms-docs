---
title: Safety-Kleen Report
description: Multi-sheet Excel reports for Safety-Kleen orders.
---

Select **Safety-Kleen Report** under **Reports**. The page generates and exports **multi-sheet Excel reports** for Safety-Kleen orders.

## Export the report

1. Choose the **Invoice Number**.
2. Select **Export to Excel**.

Safety-Kleen orders arrive through the [Safety-Kleen mail-back system](/docs/orders/order-sources). Consolidated Safety-Kleen invoices are also listed in [Consolidated Invoice](/docs/reports/consolidated-invoice).
